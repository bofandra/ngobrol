# Product vision — ngobrol.
**The game that never ends.** Ask. Discuss. Connect. Discover.

Ngobrol means informal conversation or brainstorming in Indonesian. The owner imagines a game where humanity progressively builds shared understanding through everyday conversation, with AI converting discussion into inspectable causal models.

## Origin and philosophy
1. Break a complicated problem into manageable factors while preserving the whole-system context.
2. Draw relationships to understand how factors interact, including feedback, trade-offs, time and delays.
3. Use tools when cognitive load becomes too high.
4. Collectively improve methods, models and tools over time, so the next person does not start from scratch.

The goal is not to map millions of possible connections indiscriminately; it is to surface relevant relationships within an explicit boundary, then revise as new information arrives. Make complexity more manageable, not falsely simple. A game lets people practice systems thinking without knowing its terminology first.

## Three original use cases
- **Hermit crab seeking a home**: a larger shell may protect it but weigh more; mobility and survival trade off. Other crabs contribute different experiences. The next crab inherits a living map instead of starting again.
- **Choosing transportation**: time, comfort, cost, safety, mobility needs, personal condition, traffic, time of day, weather and trip purpose change the best choice. Context-sensitive preferences matter; never stereotype based on age or gender.
- **Motorcycle/car collision and escalating anger**: distinguish observed damage from assumptions; investigate road context, fatigue, urgency, and stress as hypotheses, not diagnoses or blame. AI could ask both parties, visualize agreed/disputed facts and clarify possible next steps. This is a future facilitation scenario only once everyone is safe; it must not replace emergency help, legal/insurance procedures or qualified mediation.

## Player promise
Come to play. Stay curious. Leave the world a little better understood.
Curious everyday people, domain contributors, facilitators/educators, and topic stewards all have a role. Contributions range from small questions to meaningful discoveries. Discussions may be dormant for years and resume when someone has a new perspective. No final winner.

## Scope
MVP: text topics, discussion, AI draft map, human review, evidence, votes on factors and links, proposals, version history, XP and safety controls. Public and invite-only topics are proposed; pilot should be invite-only until moderation is ready. Later: reusable submaps, topic links, missions, simulations, native expansion and AI-assisted live facilitation.

Non-goals: adjudicating objective truth, replacing experts, deciding legal responsibility, giving clinical or other high-stakes decisions, maximizing engagement at the expense of quality, or promising instant resolution. Never imply consensus is guaranteed.

## Success
Proposed north star: weekly meaningful contributions accepted through review that improve a map or its evidence. Track first-contribution completion, revisit rate, review turnaround, evidence-backed links, visibility of disagreement, safety response times, and player comprehension of +/- polarity. Validate with users before treating targets as commitments.
